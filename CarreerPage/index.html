  • Undertaking transactional activities with Banks;

  • Working with third-party vendors;
  • Closely cooperating with staff responsible for warehouse management and goods turnover.

  • Good AS-Accountant and iiko software user and administration skills;
  • Good MS Office skills and advanced Excel skills would be considered an advantage;
  • Research and analytical thinking;
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving skills;
  • Working proficiency in the English language.
